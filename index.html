    Hills, bridges & tunnels

    Raise / Lower build terraces in steps. Buildings need level ground, so placing one flattens its footprint, and raising any tile under a building lifts the whole pad.

    Lay bridge tiles across a gap, then drop a Ramp on the tile at each end: it works out what it is climbing — an adjacent bridge deck, or the tallest uphill neighbour — and slopes up to meet it, so the road joins cleanly. Tunnel portals cut into the cliff beside them automatically.

    Ground goes below the datum too, so you can dig cuttings, quarries and sunken riverbeds — handy for making a bridge meet the road.

    A tunnel portal renders into the cliff face of whichever uphill neighbour is behind it, matching that cliff's height. Place it on the low tile at the foot of the cliff.

    Lay a freeway two tiles wide: each tile becomes one carriageway, with the central reservation on the shared edge.

    Grow the map with the Extend Map Edge tool — pick it, then click an arrow off any edge to add four tiles there.

    As in any isometric view, a steep hill can hide tiles behind it. Lower the tile in front, or pan around, to reach them.

    Seasons

    The Season menu retints ground, foliage and roofs — winter dusts everything with snow and bares the oaks, autumn turns them gold, Christmas adds eave lights. The Halloween, Christmas and Easter prop groups stay available whatever season you pick.
